## 🎨 Content Tone & Voice

### Brand Voice
- **Professional but approachable**
- **Technical but not jargon-heavy**
- **Confident but not boastful**
- **Educational but not preachy**

### Writing Guidelines
1. Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
2. Include real numbers and metrics
3. Address pain points directly
4. Provide actionable advice
5. Use Indian English conventions

### Example Phrases
- ✅ "Bill in under 5 seconds"
- ✅ "Works offline, syncs online"
- ✅ "Free forever, not just a trial"
- ❌ "Revolutionary game-changer" (too salesy)
- ❌ "Cutting-edge technology" (too vague)
