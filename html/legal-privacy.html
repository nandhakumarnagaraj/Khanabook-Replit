Legal, Privacy & Policies

At a glance: This page covers our Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy, and legal information including law of jurisdiction and contact details. KhanaBook is built by India Advocacy and we are committed to transparency, data protection, and fair practices.

1. Introduction

Welcome to KhanaBook — a product built by India Advocacy. This consolidated document outlines our Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy, and related legal information. By accessing or using KhanaBook's software, website, and services, you agree to the terms described herein.

Our commitment to security and transparency is paramount. We build our systems with privacy-by-design principles to ensure that your business data and your customers' payment information remains protected under the highest industrial standards.

2. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how KhanaBook ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our POS software, hardware, and associated services. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

3. Data Collection

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our service to you. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, business name, and physical address.
  • Transaction Data: Details of transactions made through our POS terminals, excluding full credit card numbers.
  • Hardware Metadata: Technical information about your POS hardware, including serial numbers and firmware versions.
  • Operational Logs: Usage patterns and system performance metrics to ensure uptime and stability.

Compliance Standard

KhanaBook is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PCI-DSS Level 1 security standards. We undergo annual third-party audits to verify our security posture.

4. How We Use Data

The information we collect is used to maintain the integrity of your business operations. We use this data to:

  • Identify and prevent fraudulent transactions in real-time.
  • Provide detailed analytics and reporting tools to help grow your business.
  • Process payments securely through our integrated banking partners.
  • Notify you about critical system updates or hardware maintenance requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

Your data may be shared with:

  • Payment processing networks (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, UPI, etc.)
  • Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud)
  • Regulatory bodies where required by law

7. Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and improve our service.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality, like keeping you logged in.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (using anonymised data).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as theme preference (light/dark mode).

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Refund Policy

Since KhanaBook is offered completely free of charge — with no trial periods, no expiry, and no hidden fees — the question of refunds applies only to any optional paid services or payment gateway transactions processed through our platform.

Free Services: All core KhanaBook features (billing, KOT management, UPI payments, GST reconciliation, reports, and printer support) are free forever. No refunds are applicable as no payment is collected.

Payment Gateway Transactions: Transactions processed through integrated payment gateways (including UPI, card payments, and Easebuzz) are subject to the refund policies of the respective payment gateway provider. KhanaBook acts as a facilitator and is not responsible for delays or disputes arising from third-party payment processors.

Dispute Resolution: Any billing disputes or transaction reversals must be raised directly with the payment gateway provider. KhanaBook will extend reasonable assistance in resolving such disputes.

Chargebacks: In the event of a chargeback initiated by a customer, the merchant (restaurant) agrees to cooperate with KhanaBook and the payment gateway to provide necessary transaction evidence.
