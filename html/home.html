How It Works

Menu to first bill in under 5 minutes.

No hardware to buy. No training sessions. Just three straightforward steps.

1

Snap a photo of your menu

Take a picture or upload a PDF. The AI reads it — every item, price, and category — and builds your digital menu. Takes seconds, not hours.

2

Bill in 5 seconds, not 5 minutes

Pick the items, add any modifications, take payment — UPI, cash, card, or split between guests. GST is automatic. Print or send via WhatsApp. That's it.

3

Let compliance take care of itself

Every bill is reconciled for GST automatically. You get reminders before deadlines. And from the dashboard, you can see what's selling, when you're busiest, and exactly how much you made today. Download Android App for on-the-go access.
