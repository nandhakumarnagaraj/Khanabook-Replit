Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how KhanaBook (operated by India Advocacy) collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Introduction

KhanaBook ("we," "us," or "our") is a restaurant POS platform built by India Advocacy. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our POS software, website, mobile application, and related services.

We adhere to the principles of data minimization, purpose limitation, and transparency. Our systems are designed with privacy-by-design principles to ensure your business data and your customers' payment information remains protected under the highest industrial standards.

Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, business name, and physical address when you register for an account or contact us.
  • Business Information: Restaurant name, GST number, FSSAI licence number, PAN card, business address, and menu details required for POS operations and compliance.
  • Transaction Data: Details of transactions processed through our POS system, including order items, amounts, payment methods, and timestamps. We do not store full credit/debit card numbers.
  • Device Information: Hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and Bluetooth printer configurations for optimal performance.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our application — features used, time spent, and error reports — to improve the product experience.
  • Location Information: Approximate location (city/region) derived from your IP address or device settings for compliance and localised features.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as race, religion, or political affiliation.

3. How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is used exclusively to maintain, improve, and personalise your experience with KhanaBook. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Provide Core Services: Process transactions, generate bills, manage orders, and synchronise data across devices.
  • Ensure Compliance: Automatically compute GST, generate compliance-ready reports, and assist with tax filing obligations.
  • Prevent Fraud: Identify and block fraudulent transactions in real-time using transaction pattern analysis.
  • Technical Support: Diagnose issues, provide customer support, and send critical system updates.
  • Product Improvement: Analyse aggregated usage patterns to improve features, performance, and user experience.
  • Communications: Send service-related announcements, policy updates, and compliance reminders. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our platform, conducting our business, or servicing you, provided those parties agree to keep your information confidential and comply with applicable data protection laws.

Your data may be shared with:

  • Payment Gateways: Easebuzz and other payment processors for transaction processing. We only share necessary transaction data. Full card numbers are never stored by KhanaBook.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: AWS and Google Cloud for secure data hosting and backup services.
  • Government APIs: GST portal and other regulatory bodies for compliance and tax filing purposes as required by law.
  • Analytics: Anonymised usage data may be shared with analytics providers to help us improve the product.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

6. Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and improve our service.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality, such as keeping you logged in and maintaining session state. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website using aggregated, anonymised data. We use these to improve our site and content.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as theme preference (light/dark mode) and language preferences.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. We also obtain your consent before setting non-essential cookies, in compliance with India's DPDP Act, 2023.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements (e.g., GST records must be retained as per tax laws).
  • Right to Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format for transfer to another service provider.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent to process your data. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with a data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request within 30 days as required by applicable law.
