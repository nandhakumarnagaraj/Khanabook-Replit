Refund Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026. This Refund Policy explains the terms and conditions under which refunds are processed for transactions made through the KhanaBook platform. Since our core software is free, this policy primarily governs payment gateway transactions.

1. Introduction

KhanaBook is a free restaurant POS platform. We do not charge for our software — there are no subscription fees, trial periods, or hidden costs. As such, the concept of a "refund" for software fees does not apply.

However, transactions processed through our integrated payment gateways (including Easebuzz, UPI, and card payments) may be eligible for refunds under certain conditions. This policy outlines the process, timelines, and limitations governing such refunds.

KhanaBook (operated by India Advocacy) acts as a facilitator between merchants (restaurants) and payment gateway providers. We are not a bank or financial institution and do not hold customer funds.

2. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be initiated under the following conditions:

  • Duplicate Transactions: If a customer is charged multiple times for the same order due to a technical error, the duplicate amount is eligible for a full refund.
  • Failed Transactions: If a payment is deducted from the customer's account but the transaction is not successfully completed or reflected in the merchant's account, the amount is eligible for a refund.
  • Incorrect Amount: If the customer was charged an incorrect amount (overcharged), the excess amount is eligible for a refund.
  • Order Cancellation: If the merchant cancels an order after payment has been collected, the refund is at the discretion of the merchant and subject to the merchant's own cancellation policy.
  • Service Not Provided: If the merchant fails to provide the ordered goods or services, the customer may be eligible for a refund subject to verification.

Refund requests must be initiated within 30 days of the original transaction date to be considered.

3. Cancellation Process

To request a refund or cancellation:

  • Step 1: Contact the merchant (restaurant) directly to resolve the issue. Most disputes can be resolved at this level.
  • Step 2: If the merchant is unable to resolve the issue, contact KhanaBook support at kbook@pcts.tech with your transaction details, including transaction ID, amount, date, and reason for the refund request.
  • Step 3: Our support team will review the request and, if eligible, initiate the refund process through the payment gateway provider.
  • Step 4: For chargeback requests, we will guide you through the process and provide necessary transaction evidence to the payment gateway.

Merchants may also initiate refunds directly from their KhanaBook dashboard for eligible transactions.

4. Refund Timelines

Refund processing times vary depending on the payment method and the payment gateway provider:

  • UPI Transactions: Refunds are typically processed within 3–5 business days after approval. The amount is credited back to the source UPI account.
  • Card Payments: Refunds to credit or debit cards typically take 5–10 business days, depending on the card-issuing bank's processing times.
  • Net Banking: Refunds to bank accounts typically take 3–7 business days.
  • Wallet Payments: Refunds to digital wallets are typically processed within 24–48 hours.

These timelines are estimates provided by our payment gateway partners. Actual processing times may vary based on the customer's bank or payment provider. KhanaBook is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions.

5. Exceptions

The following situations are generally not eligible for refunds:

  • Change of Mind: Refunds are not provided for a change of mind after a transaction has been successfully completed and services have been rendered.
  • Partial Service: If the customer received a portion of the ordered goods or services, the refund, if any, is at the sole discretion of the merchant.
  • Promotional Transactions: Transactions made during promotional periods may be subject to specific refund terms as communicated at the time of the promotion.
  • Third-Party Services: KhanaBook is not responsible for refunds related to third-party services integrated with the platform (e.g., food delivery aggregators). Such disputes must be handled directly with the third-party provider.
  • Fraudulent Claims: Refund requests found to be fraudulent or based on false information will be rejected and may be reported to relevant authorities.
