● Why we built this

We saw what was broken.
So we built something better.

India Advocacy has been serving Indian businesses for years — GST, FSSAI, licensing. We saw firsthand how hard it is for restaurants to find a POS that actually fits. So we built one.

rocket_launch Try it free — no credit card needed visibility What we believe

Born from real experience.

India Advocacy started as a compliance platform — helping businesses with GST filings, FSSAI licenses, shop registrations, and everything else that goes into running a legitimate business in India.

Over the years, we worked with over 1.5 lakh businesses. And one thing kept coming up: restaurant owners couldn't find a POS that worked the way their business actually runs.

Existing solutions were either too expensive, too complicated, or built for markets halfway across the world. So we built our own.

KhanaBook is the POS we wished existed when we started. Built for Indian restaurants — dhabas, cloud kitchens, cafes, bakeries, fine dining. One clean app for billing, KOT, payments, reporting, and compliance.

We didn't build KhanaBook in a boardroom. We built it because the restaurants we worked with asked for it. Every feature comes from a real problem someone faced during a dinner rush.
