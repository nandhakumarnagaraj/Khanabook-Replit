Born from India Advocacy.

India Advocacy is a leading legal-tech and business enablement platform focused on helping Indian businesses simplify compliance, operations, and digital growth at scale. Over the years, we've built deep relationships with businesses across India through a service ecosystem covering:

  • GST, ITR & TDS compliance
  • FSSAI licensing and regulatory support
  • Shop and trade licensing
  • Labour registrations and business compliance
  • Business operations and digital enablement

Today, through our merchant network, we have served and supported 1.5 lakh+ shops and businesses across India — including a rapidly growing restaurant and food-service segment. With deep operational understanding of merchant pain points, we built KhanaBook.

KhanaBook is not a conventional billing software. It is built for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, bakeries, and food businesses that need one clean place for billing, KOT, payments, reporting, and compliance.

From merchant onboarding to compliance support, India Advocacy's ecosystem was designed around the realities of running a business in India. KhanaBook brings that same operational thinking into a faster restaurant workflow.
