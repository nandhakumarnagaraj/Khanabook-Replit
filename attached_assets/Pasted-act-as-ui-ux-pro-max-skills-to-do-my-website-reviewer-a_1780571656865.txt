🔍 KhanaBook UI/UX Review

🔴 Critical Issues
1. Hero section is cut off (Homepage)
The product mockup image on the right is getting clipped — the phone/tablet mockup bleeds off-screen. Users never see the full product visual, which is your #1 trust builder.

Fix: Add overflow: hidden on the hero section or constrain the image width with max-width so it stays fully visible.

2. No social proof above the fold
The 3 stats (5s, 100%, 200+) are buried below the visible area. Restaurant owners are skeptical — they need trust signals immediately.

Fix: Move the stat strip inside the hero, right below the CTA buttons, so it's visible without scrolling.

3. CTA hierarchy conflict (Features page)
The page has two buttons — "Start First Year Free" (primary) and "See Pricing" (secondary) — but both have similar visual weight. The eye doesn't know where to go first.

Fix: Make the primary CTA clearly dominant (solid filled), secondary should be ghost/outline only — which it is, but the sizing/padding feels too equal.

🟠 High Priority Improvements
4. Navbar is too crowded on desktop
6 nav links + dark mode toggle + CTA button = cognitive overload. The "Contact" link competes with the "Start First Year Free" button, which is the main conversion goal.

Fix: Move "Help Center" and "Contact" into a secondary dropdown or footer-only. Keep nav to: Features · Pricing · Compare · About Us + CTA.

5. Homepage hero text alignment mismatch
The left side (headline + body + CTAs) is left-aligned, but the announcement pill ("NOW WITH AI MENU IMPORT") is also left-aligned but not fully aligned with the headline — it creates a slightly ragged edge on mobile viewports.

Fix: Align the pill to the same left edge as the headline text.

6. "Book a Demo" button style is ambiguous (Homepage)
It looks like a secondary action but sits equal to the Google Play badge. A restaurant owner might not know which to click first.

Fix: Reorder: Google Play first (primary action for an Android POS), then Book a Demo as secondary. Or visually separate them with a label like "Or schedule a walkthrough".

7. Contact form has no confirmation feedback
The "Get in Touch" page has a form but no visible success/error state communicated in the UI.

Fix: Add a clear success message or redirect after submission.

8. Pricing page — price reveal is below fold
The ₹0 / ₹99 pricing card is partially hidden. Users read the headline "Start free for one full year" but don't see the price card until scrolling.

Fix: Reduce the hero padding on the pricing page so the card is partially visible — this creates a "scroll prompt" and keeps users engaged.

🟡 Medium Priority Suggestions
9. Add a sticky "floating CTA" bar
As users scroll deep into Features or Compare pages, they lose the navbar CTA. A sticky bottom bar on mobile ("Start Free — No Credit Card") would significantly boost conversion.

10. Our Story page lacks a team section
The story is compelling ("Born in the kitchen") but there are no faces or names. B2B software buyers want to know who they're trusting with their business.

Add a simple 2–3 person founder/team block with photos and short bios.

11. Comparison table needs a sticky header row
On the vs-Petpooja page, as you scroll down the table, the column headers (KhanaBook, Petpooja, Posist, Loyverse) disappear. This forces users to scroll back up to recall which column is which.

Fix: position: sticky; top: 0 on the table header row.

12. No breadcrumbs or "you are here" indicator on inner pages
Pages like Features, Compare, and Our Story drop users straight into content with no sense of position in the site. The page URL badge (e.g., "Full Feature Set") helps but isn't enough.

13. Dark mode is incomplete
The dark mode toggle exists but some cards (especially on the Compare and Features pages) may show white backgrounds in dark mode — inconsistent experience.
